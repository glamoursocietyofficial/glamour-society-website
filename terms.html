03 · The Platform & Two Roles

The Two Roles

House Members (Talent) are creative professionals — models, photographers, creators, stylists, athletes, artists, DJs, makeup artists, and others — who create profiles, set their own rates, and receive bookings through the Platform. Members keep 70% of every completed booking. House of Elévara retains 30% as a platform fee covering payment processing, dispute coverage, infrastructure, and platform operations.

Patrons (Clients) are brands, businesses, agencies, and individuals who browse talent on The Floor and submit structured booking requests. Patrons pay the Member's service rate. The 30% House fee is factored into the total charged at checkout.

House of Elévara is a marketplace. We are not a party to the agreement between Members and Patrons. We facilitate the booking — we do not employ, contract, or represent either party.

Referral Program

Referring a brand Patron earns 10% of the House fee on their first 3 bookings. Referral earnings are paid out automatically through Stripe and are visible in The Ledger.
