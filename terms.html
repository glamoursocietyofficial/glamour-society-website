The Two Roles

House Members (Talent) are creative professionals — models, photographers, creators, stylists, athletes, artists, DJs, makeup artists, and others — who create profiles, set their own rates, and receive bookings through the Platform. Patrons (Clients) are brands, businesses, agencies, and individuals who browse talent on The Floor and submit structured booking requests. Patrons pay the Member's service rate. The 30% House fee is factored into the total charged at checkout.
