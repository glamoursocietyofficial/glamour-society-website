Transparency & Expectations

House of Elévara provides professional talent management services via houseofelevara.com. Membership fees support talent coordination, brand outreach, profile management, and editorial opportunities. These are website services and do not affect The Atelier app.

Participation in any paid tier does not guarantee bookings, income, or campaign selection. What it provides is dedicated talent management, ongoing coordination, and professional representation via our website platform.

The Atelier iOS app operates independently. All app features — including booking, messaging, portfolios, and earnings — are available to every user regardless of website membership status.

The Elévara Shop is live at shop.houseofelevara.com. Ambassadors earn commission on every product sold through their referral link. Commission rates and payout terms are outlined in the Ambassador Program.
