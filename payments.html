Transparency & Expectations

Membership fees support talent coordination, brand outreach, profile management, and editorial opportunities delivered through houseofelevara.com.

Participation in any paid tier does not guarantee bookings, income, or campaign selection. What it provides is dedicated talent management, ongoing coordination, and professional representation via our website platform.

The Ambassador Program is free to join and separate from the paid tiers above. Ambassadors earn 10–25% commission on completed product sales driven through their link, with rates rising by lifetime sales volume. Full terms are set out in the Ambassador Program.

The Atelier iOS app operates independently. All app features — including booking, messaging, portfolios, and earnings — are available to every user regardless of website membership or Ambassador status.
