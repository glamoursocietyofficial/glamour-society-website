What happens after you apply

  • We review your details for readiness, professionalism, and fit.
  • If approved, you’ll receive interview scheduling and next steps.

Tip: Watch your email for updates and respond quickly to move forward.

Quick tips for approval

  • Use clear, well-lit photos and accurate measurements.
  • Include your Instagram and realistic availability.
  • Be professional, responsive, and consistent.
  • Submit a clean headshot + at least one full-body image.
