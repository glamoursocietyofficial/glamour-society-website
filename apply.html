After You Apply

  • We review your submission for professionalism, readiness, and fit within the Elévara ecosystem.
  • If approved, you will receive interview scheduling and detailed onboarding next steps via email.
  • Once confirmed, onboarding covers expectations, conduct standards, and booking protocol.
  • You are then placed on our active roster and considered for opportunities as they develop.

Watch your email after submitting. Respond quickly — responsiveness is part of our standard.

Tips for Approval

  • Submit clear, well-lit photos — a clean headshot and at least one full-body image.
  • Provide accurate measurements and realistic availability.
  • Include your Instagram handle and any relevant experience or training.
  • Be professional in every field — your application is your first impression.
  • Be consistent and responsive from the moment you apply.
