This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use The Atelier mobile application and our websites, including houseofelevara.com and elyraeditorial.com (collectively, the "Platform"). By creating an account or using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform. 02 · INFORMATION WE COLLECT Information We Collect We collect information you provide directly to us and information generated through your use of the Platform. Account Information • Email address (used for authentication via one-time passcode) • Full name and display handle • City and general location • Profile photos and portfolio media • Professional biography and niche categories • Social media handles (Instagram, TikTok) — optional • Brand name and company information (Patrons only) Transaction Information • Booking details including service descriptions, dates, and briefs • Payment amounts and payout history • Stripe Connect account identifiers (we do not store full card numbers) • Reference images attached to booking briefs Usage Information • Device type, operating system, and app version • Pages and features accessed within the Platform • Referral codes used and generated • Messages sent within The Salon (stored to facilitate your bookings) Automatically Collected Information • Session tokens stored locally via device storage (UserDefaults/Keychain) to keep you signed in • Notification preferences stored locally on your device 03 · HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve the Platform. Specifically: • To create and manage your account and authenticate your identity via OTP • To display your profile on The Floor for discovery by Patrons • To facilitate bookings between Members and Patrons • To process payments and disburse earnings through Stripe Connect • To send transactional emails including OTP codes and booking confirmations via Resend • To send push notifications about bookings, payouts, and referral earnings • To calculate and display earnings, referrals, and tier progression • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries • To enforce our Terms of Service and prevent fraud or abuse • To comply with legal obligations We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes on behalf of third-party advertisers. 04 · HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION How We Share Information We share your information only in the following limited circumstances: With Other Users Your public profile information — including your name, handle, city, niche tags, portfolio media, rate card, and tier — is visible to other users on The Floor and on your profile page. Patron identities are not displayed publicly on The Floor. With Service Providers • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure • Stripe — payment processing and Member payouts via Stripe Connect Express • Resend — transactional email delivery • Cloudflare — webhook processing and platform infrastructure Each service provider is bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements. We do not authorize them to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing services to us. For Legal Reasons We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others. Business Transfers In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email or prominent notice on the Platform before your information is transferred. 05 · PAYMENTS & FINANCIAL DATA Payments & Financial Data All payment processing on The Atelier is handled by Stripe, Inc. When you connect your bank account as a House Member, you complete Stripe's Connect Express onboarding directly with Stripe. House of Elévara does not store your full bank account numbers, routing numbers, or card numbers. We retain records of transaction amounts, booking IDs, payout dates, and Stripe account identifiers for accounting, tax reporting, and dispute resolution purposes. Members who earn more than $600 in a calendar year through the Platform will receive IRS Form 1099-NEC from Stripe or House of Elévara LLC as required by law. By using the Platform, you consent to electronic delivery of tax documents. Stripe's privacy policy governs the collection and use of your financial data during payment processing. You can review Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy. 06 · DATA STORAGE & SECURITY Data Storage & Security Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the United States. We implement industry-standard security measures including: • Encrypted data transmission via HTTPS/TLS • Row-level security policies on all database tables • Authentication tokens stored in device Keychain • One-time passcode authentication — no passwords stored • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure Despite our efforts, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (such as financial records, which we retain for 7 years). 07 · YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES Your Rights & Choices You have the following rights regarding your personal information: • Access — You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you • Correction — You may update your profile information at any time within the app via Settings • Deletion — You may delete your account and associated data at any time (see Account Deletion below) • Portability — You may request an export of your data in a machine-readable format • Notifications — You may manage push notification preferences within the app Settings • Opt-out — You may opt out of non-essential communications by contacting us To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@houseofelevara.com. We will respond to all requests within 30 days. If you are a resident of California, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). If you are a resident of the European Economic Area, you may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Please contact us to exercise these rights. 08 · ACCOUNT DELETION Account Deletion You may delete your House of Elévara account at any time directly within The Atelier app: • Open The Atelier app • Tap More in the bottom navigation • Tap Settings • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Account • Confirm deletion in the alert that appears Account deletion permanently removes your profile, archive, rate card, availability settings, and all associated data from our active systems. Any pending bookings will be cancelled. Completed booking records may be retained for legal and financial compliance purposes for up to 7 years. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you wish to use The Atelier again after deleting your account, you will need to create a new account. You may also request account deletion by emailing admin@houseofelevara.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. 09 · CHILDREN'S PRIVACY Children's Privacy The Atelier is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 18 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe we may have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately at admin@houseofelevara.com. 10 · CHANGES TO THIS POLICY Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors.
