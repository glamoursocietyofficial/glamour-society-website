Recognition · Not a Campaign · Named, Not Hired

Are you a Vorane?

We are not building a brand that chases its audience. We are building a brand that names a kind of person who already exists — and then gives her the products, the scent, and the room she has been quietly looking for.

Origin

We do not catch trends. We name a sensibility that has been moving through rooms long before we put a word on it.

Gravity

A Vorane alters a room by entering it. Quiet authority. Loud is the opposite of Vorane.

Becoming

A Vorane is not made by us — she is recognized by us. The brand is the mirror, not the maker.

A Vorane is not a face we hired. She is the figure the room turns toward — before she enters.

Named, Not BookedVoranes are recognized by the house — not hired from a casting call. The bar is presence, not portfolio.

The Elévara Foundation

The Elévara Foundation is the nonprofit arm of the House — a 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to removing financial barriers for emerging creative talent. We believe the next generation of models, artists, and creators shouldn't be held back by access to capital, equipment, or mentorship. The Foundation exists to change that.

🎨
Portfolio Grants
Funding for emerging talent to build professional portfolios — photography, design, and creative production costs covered.
💼
Business Capital
Seed capital for creatives launching independent brands, studios, or agencies within the fashion and entertainment space.
🎤
Equipment Access
Grants for cameras, lighting, audio, and production equipment — giving emerging talent the tools professionals already have.
🏛️
Mentorship Program
Direct access to established industry professionals for guidance on career development, brand building, and business strategy.
📚
Education & Workshops
Free and subsidized workshops on industry business knowledge — contracts, rates, rights, and how to build a sustainable creative career.
🤝
Community Support
Building a network of creatives who support each other — connecting emerging talent with working professionals and real opportunities.
100% to Programs

Every dollar donated to the Elévara Foundation goes directly to programs and recipients — not overhead. EIN pending. Launching June 2026.
