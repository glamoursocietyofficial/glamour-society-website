Frequently Asked

Quick answers for talent and clients.

How do I apply?

Apply through the Apply page. If selected, we schedule an interview and share onboarding next steps.

How do bookings work?

Use Request a Booking or email us directly. We confirm availability, details, and expectations with full documentation.

Do you offer brand partnerships and campaigns?

Yes. We coordinate brand campaigns and placements with a structured process and clearly defined deliverables for both parties.

What is Elyra Editorial?

Elyra Editorial is our connected media and editorial presence. Visit elyraeditorial.com to explore our editorial direction.

Is joining free?

Yes. The roster is free to join. Optional paid membership tiers are available for talent who wants priority consideration, active development support, and ambassador earning opportunities. View membership options.

Can I view and book specific models?

Yes. Visit our Talent Roster to browse active models, view full stats, and submit a booking inquiry directly from each profile.
