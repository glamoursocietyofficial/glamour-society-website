Frequently Asked

Quick answers for talent and clients.

How do I apply?

Apply through the Apply page. If selected, we schedule an interview and share onboarding next steps.

How do bookings work?

Use Request a Booking or email us directly. We confirm availability, details, and expectations with full documentation.

Do you offer brand partnerships and campaigns?

Yes. We coordinate brand campaigns and placements with a structured process and clearly defined deliverables for both parties.

What is Elyra Editorial?

Elyra Editorial is our connected media and editorial presence. Visit elyraeditorial.com.

Is joining free?

Yes. The roster is free to join. Optional paid membership tiers are available. View membership options.

What is The Atelier app?

The Atelier is our iOS booking platform coming soon to the App Store. Set your rate, get discovered, and keep 70% of every booking. Learn more.

What is the Collection?

The inaugural House of Elévara collection — four pieces releasing in sequence. Fragrance and leather goods designed with intention. View the collection.

Can I view and book specific models?

Yes. Visit our Talent Roster to browse active models and submit a booking inquiry from each profile.

Can I schedule a private call before purchasing?

Yes. We strongly recommend scheduling an appointment before any membership, ambassador activation, or collection purchase. Book a call here.
