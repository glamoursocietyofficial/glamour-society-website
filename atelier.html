How It Works
01
Build Your Profile

Create your House Member profile in minutes. Upload your portfolio to The Archive, set your services and rates on your Rate Card, and mark your availability on My Calendar.

02
Get Discovered on The Floor

Your profile lives on The Floor — a curated discovery feed browsed by brands and clients. They filter by city, niche, tier, and rate to find exactly the talent they need.

03
Accept Bookings in The Salon

When a Patron books you they submit a professional brief. You review it in The Salon, accept or decline, and all communication stays in one documented thread.

04
Deliver & Get Paid

Complete the work. 70% of your booking value is released directly to your connected bank account via Stripe. Cash out instantly from The Ledger anytime.

05
Browse Opportunities

Beyond direct bookings, The Opportunities feed shows open casting calls, brand ambassador roles, UGC campaigns, and retainer agreements posted by Patrons.

06
Grow Your Tier & Earnings

Every booking builds your tier — Rising, Pro, Elite, Inner Circle. Higher tiers mean more visibility on The Floor, better positioning, and access to The Vault wealth tools.
