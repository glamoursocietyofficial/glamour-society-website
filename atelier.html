How It Works
01
Download & Build Your Profile

Download The Atelier free from the App Store. Create your House Member profile in minutes — set a profile photo, upload your portfolio to The Archive, set your services and rates on your Rate Card, and mark your availability.

02
Get Discovered on The Floor

Your profile lives on The Floor — a curated discovery feed browsed by brands and clients. They filter by country, city, niche, tier, and rate to find exactly the talent they need.

03
Accept Bookings in The Salon

When a Patron books you they submit a professional brief. You review it in The Salon, accept or decline, and all communication stays in one documented thread.

04
Deliver & Get Paid

Complete the work. 70% of your booking value is released to your connected bank account through Stripe. No invoice to chase. No follow-up email to write.

05
Browse Opportunities

Beyond direct bookings, The Opportunities feed shows open casting calls, brand ambassador roles, UGC campaigns, and retainer agreements posted by Patrons.

06
Pitch Brands with The Studio

The Studio is your AI creative director. Discover brands actively hiring in your niche, generate personalized pitch messages, and ask anything about strategy, rates, or industry trends. 50 free credits every month.

07
Grow Your Tier & Earnings

Every booking builds your tier — Rising, Pro, Elite, Inner Circle. Higher tiers mean more visibility on The Floor, and The Vault unlocks once you have earned $5,000.
