Women · Miami, Florida · Creative Director · By Selection Only

Leora Slater directs the visual language of House of Elévara. Every campaign, every editorial frame, every object that carries the house name passes through her eye before it reaches the world. She now joins the roster as talent by her own choosing — not hired into the frame, but stepping into the one she normally builds. That combination is rare: a creative director who understands the shot from both sides of the lens, and who arrives on set already fluent in the intention behind it. Based in Miami. Booked selectively.

She is not a face the House hired. She is the eye the House trusts — now standing in front of the lens instead of behind it.

I. Direction

Creative Director of House of Elévara. Sets the visual standard across campaign, editorial, and product — the house aesthetic runs through her.

II. Presence

She books as talent because the frame calls for her, not because a slot needed filling. Selective by design — a limited number of engagements per season.

III. Advantage

Clients book a model who already thinks like a director. No translation required between concept and execution — she arrives understanding the brief.
