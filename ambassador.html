Frequently Asked
Does it really cost nothing to join?

Yes. There is no activation fee, no monthly cost, and no requirement to purchase anything. You earn on what you sell, not on what you buy.

How do I reach a higher tier?

Tiers are based on lifetime sales driven through your link. Reach $2,500 and your rate becomes 15%. Reach $10,000 and it becomes 25%. Your rate never decreases.

When and how am I paid?

Commission is calculated on completed, non-refunded orders and paid on a monthly cycle. Payout details are confirmed during onboarding.

What happens if a customer returns an item?

Commission is earned on completed sales. If an order is refunded, the associated commission is reversed. This protects both the Ambassador and the House.

Do I need a large following?

No. Ambassadors range from working models to stylists to people who simply know the right rooms. What matters is that the people you reach would genuinely want these pieces.

Can I be an Ambassador and roster talent at the same time?

Yes. The two are independent. Roster talent are welcome to become Ambassadors, and Ambassador status has no effect on representation or bookings.

What am I actually selling?

The inaugural House of Elévara collection — N° 01 The Parfum is available now, with The Evening, The Tote, and The Cardholder arriving in sequence. View the full collection.

Is there a contract?

Yes. Ambassadors receive a short written agreement covering commission terms, brand usage, and conduct. There is no minimum term and no exclusivity requirement.
